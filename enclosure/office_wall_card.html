OFFICE INTERCOM what to do when it makes a sound

It is making a sound

Ringing every 2 secondsA room number blinks on the display.

That room is calling the office.

On the office page the room is blue. Click it, then hold Hold to talk to room … and speak. Only that room hears you. Not answered: the ringing stops after 10 seconds.

Ringing again every 30 secondsThe same room number blinks.

A call for help is still waiting.

The office page shows the room and what to do. Talk to the room or send someone. Then press Help is on the way: close this call.

You hear a room, with no ringing firstNobody is speaking to you.

A silent call for help. You hear that room's microphone. The room cannot hear you.

The office page shows which room. Follow the steps on the page.

Two short beeps every 30 secondsNo number blinks.

The office page is not open on any PC.

Open it in the browser (the address is at the bottom of this sheet). The beeps stop in a few seconds.

PC not working? Hold OFFICE on the intercom for 3 seconds, until it chimes. The beeps stay quiet for 1 hour, then start again. Calls, calls for help and alarms still sound. Get the PC fixed soon: the page shows who is calling and why.
Alarm signalThe display shows LoC EUA or SHE

Lockdown, evacuate or shelter. Every intercom is already sounding it.

The office page turns red and lists the steps. Follow them.

To end it: on the office page only, False alarm: all clear, with the web password. No intercom can end it.

Talking

TALK Hold and speak: the whole school hears you. Let go when you have finished.

One room: on the office page, click the room and hold Hold to talk to room …

Emergency

RED Lift the lid and press: the whole school goes to lockdown at once.

Or on the office page: Lockdown, Evacuate or Shelter.
