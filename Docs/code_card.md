# Intercom code card

To print and put next to each unit. A print-ready version is [code_card.html](code_card.html) (open it in a
browser and print; it fits half an A4 or Letter page).

The front has two buttons: **TALK** (the whole school) and **OFFICE**.

**Talk to the school:** hold **TALK** and speak. Let go when you've finished.

**Answer the office:** when it chimes, **`CAL`** flashes and the white and blue lights blink, hold **OFFICE** (or TALK)
and speak: only the office hears you. Missed it? `CAL` alternates with the room number and the lights blink briefly
every 2 s: press **OFFICE** to call the office back (any button clears it).

**Call the office:** press **OFFICE**. The display alternates **`oFC`** and the room number until the office calls
back; press OFFICE again if you need help (`HLP`).

| Display | Means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| `214` | Working | – |
| dot after 1st digit | You're talking | – |
| dot after 2nd digit | Someone is talking to you | – |
| `CAL` | The office is calling this room | Hold TALK to answer |
| `CAL` / room number, lights blink every 2 s | A call nobody answered (up to 5 min) | Press OFFICE to call the office back (any button clears it) |
| `oFC` / number | This room is calling the office | Wait for the office |
| `E1` | Network cable problem, on Wi-Fi | Tell the office |
| `E2` | No network | Tell the office |
| `Lo` | Battery low | Plug in the PoE cable |
| `oL` | A light / accessory cable is shorted | Tell the office |
| `dUP` / number | Two units with one number | Tell the office |
| `---` | No room number | Tell the office |

`CAL` and `oFC` come with firmware R2.
