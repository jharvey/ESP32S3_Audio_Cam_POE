# Classroom intercom: teacher's guide

One page for the people who use the intercom every day. Features marked *(planned)* are on the roadmap but
not in the units yet.

## Talking to the school

- **Hold TALK and speak.** Every room hears you while you hold it. Let go when you've finished.
- Any of the TALK buttons works; use whichever is easiest to reach.
- Speak from about an arm's length away, at a normal voice. The unit evens out loud and quiet voices.
- Several people can talk at once; everyone hears up to the four loudest.
- There's no time limit: in an emergency you can hold TALK as long as you need.

## What the display shows

| Display | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Room number (e.g. `214`) | Working normally | Nothing |
| A dot after the first digit | You're talking | |
| A dot after the second digit | Someone is talking to you | |
| `E1` flashing briefly | Network cable not connected; the unit is on Wi-Fi | Tell the office or IT |
| `E2` flashing briefly | No network at all | Tell the office or IT |
| `Lo` flashing briefly | Battery low (portable units) | Plug it into its PoE cable |
| `oL` flashing briefly | A light or accessory cable on the unit is shorted; that output was switched off | Tell the office or IT; the intercom still works |
| `dUP` and the number alternating | Another unit has the same room number | Tell the office; one unit needs its number changed |
| `---` | No room number set yet | Set the number (see below) |
| `CAL` *(planned)* | The office is calling your room | Hold TALK to answer |
| `ALr` *(planned)* | Lockdown / emergency | Follow the school's emergency plan |

A light on the unit *(planned)* pulses slowly for a call to your room and flashes for an emergency.

## Setting the room number (new or replacement unit)

1. Take the front cover off.
2. Hold the small **SETUP** button inside for 3 seconds. The first digit blinks.
3. Press **UP** or **DOWN** to change the blinking digit, **MODE** to move to the next digit.
4. Press **MODE** on the last digit to save. The unit restarts on its new number.
5. Put the cover back. The number stays, even through power cuts.

If the display then shows `dUP`, another unit already uses that number: pick the right one.

## Calling the office *(planned)*

Press the CALL button: only the office hears it, not the whole school.
